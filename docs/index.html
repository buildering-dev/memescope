Do "smart money" wallets persist?

Written down on 7 Oct 2026 before any data existed. Remove insiders, rank the rest by realised profit in launch weeks 1–2, take the top 1 %, and check in weeks 3–4 whether they beat the median wallet at 95 % confidence and whether copying them after a realistic delay is net positive after fees. The answer is published either way.

Status: waiting for 28 full launch days (have 5). Expected result: early November 2026.
